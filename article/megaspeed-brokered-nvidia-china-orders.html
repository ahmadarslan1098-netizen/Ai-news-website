Megaspeed brokered nearly $12 billion in Nvidia chip sales to China

A broker owned by former CEO Jensen Huang's ex-partner reportedly handled billions in mainland processor sales as analysts query official revenue figures.

By Dev Okafor · Nov 15, 2026

Megaspeed, an entity owned by ousted Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang's former partner Alice Huang, arranged nearly $12 billion in Nvidia chip orders for Chinese enterprises between 2024 and 2025, according to a report from The Wall Street Journal. The disclosures arrive as the semiconductor manufacturer faces escalating regulatory scrutiny and leadership turbulence following Huang's recent resignation from the company.

The scale of the transactions has intensified questions regarding Nvidia's reported commercial exposure to mainland China. Short-selling research firm Culper Research estimates that Nvidia's actual revenue share derived from China exceeds 20 percent, substantially above the 7 percent officially reported by the chipmaker. Lawmakers have cited the intermediary transactions in renewed calls to investigate whether broker networks bypassed federal export restrictions on advanced processors.
