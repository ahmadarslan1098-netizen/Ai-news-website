Anthropic raises $81.3 billion in record Nasdaq market debut

Anthropic began trading on the Nasdaq after raising $81.3 billion in an initial public offering that valued the frontier laboratory near $1.5 trillion.

By Mara Ellison · Nov 19, 2026

Anthropic completed the largest initial public offering on record Thursday, raising $81.3 billion as it listed shares on the Nasdaq exchange under the ticker symbol ANT. The initial offering priced at $83 per share, surpassing prior capital-raising records set by SpaceX and establishing an opening market valuation of approximately $1.5 trillion for the artificial intelligence company.

Trading commenced at $111.50 per share, roughly 33 percent above the final prospectus price. During an opening bell address at the exchange, chief executive Dario Amodei said the public listing would not alter the company's commitment to safety pacing and testing standards, despite rising compute commitments with major cloud partners including Amazon and Google.

The market debut follows a rapid transition to public markets less than a month after Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 registration statement. Based on trading levels, equity set aside for the company's Public Benefit Foundation, along with employee donations and corporate matching funds, is projected to deliver up to $156 billion toward charitable initiatives.
