Tighter US export controls halt chip shipments to Chinese AI firms

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. and Samsung have stopped delivering advanced silicon to Chinese customers as regional infrastructure partnerships unravel.

By Lena Fischer · Nov 19, 2026

Contract chipmakers Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. and Samsung Electronics have halted advanced semiconductor shipments to Chinese artificial intelligence firms following intensified export restrictions from the United States government. The immediate cutoff has curtailed access to leading-edge manufacturing capacity for mainland foundation model developers.

The regulatory tightening has begun dismantling cross-border commercial arrangements. Chinese developer Moonshot AI walked away from a $150 million deployment agreement in Indonesia, while overseas cloud computing vendors have concurrently moved to restrict remote server access for mainland entities seeking alternative compute capacity.

The supply measures follow a New York Times investigation revealing that Chinese technology company Alibaba used intermediary networks to route restricted Nvidia hardware to Chinese military researchers. The disclosures have prompted tighter compliance scrutiny across international foundries and regional data centers.
